The goal of the program is to make it easy to simulate compound interest investments, bills, debts and others.

The program will ask you a couple of things:
    1- The time you want to apply your investment.
    2- The amount you want to invest.
    3- The annual interest of your investment. 
        You can simulate using a fixed interest rate or the CDI (Certificado de Depósito Interbancário) and IPCA (Brazil's CPI)
    
The program will show you a couple of things:
    1- The final amount.
    2- The profit of your investment.
    3- The last 'monthly-profit' of your investment.
